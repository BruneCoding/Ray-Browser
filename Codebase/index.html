Frequently asked questions

Why use Ray instead of Chrome or Opera GX?

Ray is the first browser built specifically for web gaming. Instantly discover and play top browser games through a dedicated portal, multitask with built-in splitscreen, and get up to 43.5%* better performance than Chrome.

Is Ray safe, secure and private?

Yes. Ray is built on the Chromium engine, is based in Finland and fully complies with the EU’s GDPR. Ray does not sell or store personalized data for advertising or third-party monetization.

Why is my antivirus or firewall blocking Ray?

Some antivirus tools flag new apps by mistake. Allowlist Ray, or temporarily disable your antivirus, run the installer, and then turn protection back on.

Do I need an account to use Ray?

No. Some games may require an account, but that is handled by the game itself, not by Ray.

Are games on Ray free?

All browser games on Ray are free to play. Some offer in-game purchases, and Amazon Luna and Xbox cloud streaming need their own paid accounts.

How far along is Ray in development?

Ray is in public beta. Share your suggestions with us on Discord and help shape what comes next.

*43.5% is based on Ray’s MotionMark WebGL benchmark compared with Chrome. Results vary by device. FPS counters above are illustrative of typical gameplay.
